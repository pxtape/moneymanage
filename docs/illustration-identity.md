# MONEYSPACE illustration identity

The user's character-design sheet is the canonical reference for all future artwork. Preserve this identity instead of inventing a different mascot or illustration treatment.

- Pale butter yellow duck; small orange oval beak; happy curved eyes; soft round blush cheeks.
- Ivory hood with two short, rounded ears. Avoid elongated rabbit ears or pink inner ears.
- Brown gingham scarf whenever the neck is visible.
- Thin, gently irregular cocoa pencil/ink outlines, soft watercolor fills, restrained paper texture. Avoid heavy crayon outlines, saturated colors, flat geometric mascot replacements, or 3D rendering.
- Chunky, rounded, irregular hand-lettered MONEYSPACE wordmark with a tiny sprout and yellow underline. Subtitle: Little duck, little steps.
- Flowers, seedlings and hearts use the same drawn line and muted palette. Let the duck perch on, hold, or lean against page edges instead of floating unrelated to the UI.
- Keep financial labels and amounts as accessible live text. Decorative illustrated lettering may use an image with an accessible heading.

Palette: ivory #FFF8E9, butter #FCE588, blush #FAD7DB, sage #B7C9A7, cocoa #6B4F3F, warm grey #E8DFD6. The retained application theme is cream; blush is an illustration accent, not a separate pink theme.

## Expanded illustration set

Twelve new poses supplement the twelve retained poses. They are not replacements for the original artwork library. The budget picker keeps its original ten choices and adds twelve new choices, stored through the same existing icon field.

| New pose | Placement and role |
| --- | --- |
| record | Expense entry, receipt writing, loading a ledger |
| receive | Income entry menu and income amount field |
| deposit | Savings action and integrated jar scene on savings cards |
| organize | Budget heading, new budget default, selectable budget picture |
| meter | Moving gauge marker, add-button companion, selected calendar date |
| calendar | Calendar panel border and selectable budget picture |
| grow | Savings empty state and selectable savings/budget picture |
| commute | Travel-category amount entry and selectable travel budget picture |
| noodles | Food-category amount entry and selectable food budget picture |
| rest | A day with no records |
| celebrate | Confirmed entry status and completed savings goal |
| search | Failed ledger loading/retry and selectable budget picture |

The 24 new painted category props cover every existing standard category (salad shares the food-bowl illustration). Stable category identifiers, saved historical names, income category codes and financial values are unchanged. Six new garden motifs replace geometric decorations: flower cluster, sprouts, hearts, leaf sprig, sprout coins, and daisy with a heart. Garden accents sit at page and panel edges rather than covering controls.

All new source PNGs remain in the generated-image library. The app uses transparent WebP encodings and regular sprite cells for responsive rendering. Do not stretch a non-square sprite viewport: put the square illustration inside a clipped or positioned wrapper instead.

The six painted navigation icons (home, monthly wallet, insights, comparison, settings, records) complete the main navigation. Their accessible text labels and button behavior remain live HTML. Standard control marks such as close, plus and direction arrows remain simple for readability.
