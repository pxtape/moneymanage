# Cream garden UI cleanup

The daily view now shows only the transaction count and omits the daily-target help text. Weekly view no longer includes the chart helper or share poster. Monthly transactions remain accessible through the daily Home view instead of being repeated on the budget page.

Budget cards are small squares with one mascot. Edit a budget to choose among ten duck illustrations; selection persists through the existing versioned budget operation. Existing color and cover metadata are preserved. Financial amounts and historical records are unchanged.

Monthly planning uses four concise forecast labels and source chips. Savings appears before budgets. The allocation donut has a simple color/name legend; selecting a segment or legend item shows its value. The app uses the cream theme only, including devices with an older dark/pink preference. Garden accents sit on panel borders, and the mobile entry button is centered independently of the unequal navigation groups.

Verification covers artwork selection and reload, historical income/expense edits from Home, versioned budget edits/deletion, forecast persistence and deficits, donut selection, cream preference reset, and mobile button geometry. Public-site browser checks use mocked financial responses; no private records are created by the tests.
